## Get Involved

At AfriSAF, we believe Africa has the agricultural resources, talent, and innovation needed to contribute meaningfully to the global transition towards sustainable energy.

We are building technology and supply-chain solutions that connect agricultural producers, feedstock suppliers, processors, logistics partners, and global energy markets to unlock the potential of sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) and other renewable energy opportunities.

We welcome developers, researchers, agricultural innovators, sustainability advocates, and technology partners who share our vision of building a more sustainable and connected energy ecosystem for Africa.

Whether you're interested in contributing to our technology, collaborating on innovative solutions, or exploring opportunities within our ecosystem, there are several ways to get involved.

- [Explore AfriSAF](https://afrisaf.com/)
- [Learn about sustainable aviation fuel](https://afrisaf.com/)
- [Explore our open-source projects](https://github.com/afrisaf)
- [Connect with AfriSAF on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/afrisaf/)

For questions, suggestions, bug reports, or contributions relating to a specific repository, please use that repository's GitHub Issues rather than contacting us through general channels.

Visit [afrisaf.com](https://afrisaf.com/) to learn more about our mission and what we're building.

---

AfriSAF is committed to building technology and solutions that promote sustainability, transparency, collaboration, and positive environmental and economic impact.

We expect everyone participating in our development communities to engage respectfully and constructively.

Our projects adopt a code of conduct that promotes collaboration, respect, and inclusivity. Please refer to the individual repository's `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` for applicable guidelines.
